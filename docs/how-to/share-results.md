# Share the teams

This guide shows you how to send the teams to a group chat or copy them as text. First [create the teams](create-teams.md).

## Share to WhatsApp or another app

1. Under the teams, select **Share**.
2. Choose where to send the teams:
   - On a phone, your device's share sheet opens. Choose WhatsApp, Viber, Messages or any other app.
   - On most computers, a **Share** window opens with **WhatsApp** and **Copy**. Select **WhatsApp** to open a WhatsApp message with the teams (WhatsApp Web on a computer).
3. In the app you chose, pick the chat and send the message.

## Copy the teams as text

To paste the teams anywhere, select **Copy** under the teams. TeamSync shows **Teams copied ✓**, and you can paste the text into any chat, email or document.

The copied text looks like the following example:

```text
✨ TEAMS GENERATED ✨

🏆 Team 1 | Members - 2
  👤 Maria
  👤 Tom

🏆 Team 2 | Members - 2
  👤 Elena
  👤 Kenji

— Made with TeamSync · https://simeontsvetanov.github.io/teamsync-random-team-generator/
```

If copying fails, TeamSync shows **Couldn't copy — select the teams and copy them by hand.** Select the team names on the page and copy them with your device's copy command.

The names leave your device only when you send or paste them. After that, the app you chose handles them under its own privacy terms.
