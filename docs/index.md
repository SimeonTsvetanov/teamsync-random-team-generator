# TeamSync user documentation

TeamSync splits any list of names into fair, random teams. It's free, runs in your browser and works offline. Open it at [simeontsvetanov.github.io/teamsync-random-team-generator](https://simeontsvetanov.github.io/teamsync-random-team-generator/).

## How-to guides

The following guides each cover one task:

- [Create teams](how-to/create-teams.md) — turn a list of names into 2 to 10 random teams.
- [Share the teams](how-to/share-results.md) — send the teams to WhatsApp or any chat, or copy them.
- [Keep or clear your names list](how-to/manage-names-list.md) — reuse last week's list or start empty.
- [Install TeamSync and use it offline](how-to/install-app.md) — open TeamSync like an app, with or without internet.
- [Make teams with an AI agent](how-to/make-teams-with-ai-agent.md) — let an AI client call TeamSync through MCP.
