# Settings and limits

This page lists every option in TeamSync's **Settings** and the limits on names and teams. To open **Settings**, go to **Menu** > **Settings**.

## Settings

The following options appear in **Settings**, in the order of the window.

| Option | What it does | Values | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Theme** | Sets the colours of TeamSync. The theme button in the header switches it too. | **System**, **Light**, **Dark** | **System** (follows your device) |
| **Language** | Sets the language of the app. | English, Български | English |
| **Space used** | Shows how much storage TeamSync uses in this browser. | A size, or **Not available in this browser** | — |
| **Kept permanently** | Shows whether the browser keeps TeamSync's data when space runs low. | **Yes ✓**, **Not yet** | **Not yet** |
| **Keep data on this device** | Asks the browser to keep TeamSync's data permanently. The browser can refuse. | — | — |
| **Export my data** | Downloads a file with everything TeamSync saved on this device, as a backup. | — | — |
| **Import data** | Replaces the saved data with an exported file, after you confirm. Files from other apps are refused. | Export file, up to 50 MB | — |
| **Clear my data** | Deletes everything TeamSync saved on this device, after you confirm. Other apps aren't affected. This can't be undone. | — | — |

## Main screen options

| Option | What it does | Values | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Names** | The people to split, one per line. Blank lines are ignored; duplicate names are allowed. | Up to 1,000 names of up to 100 characters each | Your last list, or empty |
| **Teams** | Number of teams, changed with **−** and **+**. | 2–10 | Your last choice, or 2 |

TeamSync saves the **Names** list and the **Teams** choice on this device automatically.

## Limits

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Names | 2 to 1,000, and at least as many names as teams |
| Characters in one name | 100 |
| Teams | 2 to 10 |
| Difference between team sizes | At most 1; bigger teams come first |
