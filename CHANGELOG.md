# Changelog

All notable user-facing changes. Format: Keep a Changelog · SemVer.

## [Unreleased]
### Added
- TeamSync rebuilt from scratch as a fast, installable app that works offline.
- Fair teams: every split is equally likely, team sizes differ by at most one.
- Remembers your last list and number of teams on this device.
- English and Bulgarian (Settings → Language).
- Share the teams to WhatsApp or any app; Copy gives a ready text for any chat.
- Paste a list from a chat — numbers, bullets and emoji are removed automatically.
- On Android: share a list from WhatsApp straight into TeamSync (after installing).
- Links like ?teams=3 open TeamSync with 3 teams selected.
- AI agents can make teams too (local MCP tool make_teams).
